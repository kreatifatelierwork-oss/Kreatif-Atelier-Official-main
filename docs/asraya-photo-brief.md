# Asraya — photo brief

The photos the redesigned Asraya pages need: 19 in total, 12 of them essential. In the
"Need" column, ★ means essential and "opt." means optional (the page works without it and
falls back to type).

**For every photo**
- Real NGO fieldwork, not stock. No watermarks.
- Natural light, candid and documentary in tone. Colour is fine, because the page will tone
  every photo to a warm black and white so they read as one set.
- At least 2400 px on the long edge.
- Consent from everyone recognisable, and especially for children (the Asraya copy itself
  promises "filmed with care and with their consent").
